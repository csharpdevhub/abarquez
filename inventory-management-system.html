System capabilities

Everything needed to manage inventory

The system is designed around common business inventory workflows, giving users a centralized environment for managing products, stock, suppliers, purchasing and reporting.

▦

Product Management

Manage product information, SKU numbers, categories, pricing, suppliers, stock levels, barcodes and product status.

⇅

Stock Monitoring

Monitor available inventory, reorder levels, stock movement and low-stock conditions through a centralized dashboard.

◫

Purchase Management

Create and track purchase orders, supplier transactions, received quantities and purchase costs.

♧

Supplier Management

Maintain supplier profiles, contact details, purchase history and supplier-related inventory information.

User Access Control

Support different user roles and permissions to help keep administrative functions and business data controlled.
